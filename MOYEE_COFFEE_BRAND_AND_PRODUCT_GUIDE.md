## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Essence

**Moyee Coffee** is an Amsterdam-founded, internationally operating pioneer in ethical specialty coffee, recognized globally as the world's first **FairChain** coffee company. Founded in 2012 by **Guido van Staveren van Dijk** (with the underlying FairChain Foundation established in 2010), Moyee sets out to radically overturn the conventional "Big Coffee" multinational paradigm.

### Core Taglines & Value Propositions
- *"Radically good coffee with radical impact."*
- *"Welcome to the future of coffee — a world in which everybody wins."*
- *"FairChain. Geen bullshit. Wel bizar lekkere koffie."* (Dutch brand slogan)
- *"Trade, not aid. Profitable farmers, not poor farmers."*
- *"Storyproving instead of storytelling."*
- *"No data, no claim."*
- *"Make every sip count."*

### The Name "Moyee"
In Amharic (Ethiopian language), **Moyee** (pronounced *"Mo-yeah"*) means **mortar** — the traditional carved-wood mortar and pestle used by Ethiopian families to hand-grind freshly roasted beans. The mortar is a direct symbol of the technological and economic chasm between coffee producers and western consumers. Moyee adopted this name to revolutionize and bridge that divide.

## 4. The FairChain Mission & Business Model

### The Broken Status Quo ("Big Coffee Multinationals")
- In the global coffee industry, **90% to 98% of the economic value** of every cup is extracted by multinational roasters and traders in the West.
- Coffee-growing nations export raw green beans for pennies per pound, capturing almost none of the value-add (roasting, blending, packaging, branding).
- Traditional certifications (Fairtrade, Rainforest Alliance) offer small price premiums (1–5%) but leave the extractive supply-chain structure intact.

### The FairChain Solution: 50/50 Value Split
Moyee is founded on a **50/50 joint venture model**:
1. **50% of the economic value** remains in the coffee-growing community.
2. Rather than shipping raw green coffee to European ports, Moyee **roasts and packages at origin**.
3. **Local Ownership**: Local partners and entrepreneurs co-own the processing business.

### The Three Pillars: "Factories, Farmers, Forests"

#### 1. FACTORIES (Roasting at Origin)
Moyee built cutting-edge roasting facilities in Africa:
- **Addis Ababa Roastery (Ethiopia)**:
  - Established in 2012 (foundational FairChain site).
  - 400 m² ISO-certified modern roastery.
  - Roasts ~50,000 to 77,000 kg annually.
  - Acts as an incubator and academy for female leadership and local roasting entrepreneurs.
- **Kericho Roastery (Kenya)**:
  - Unveiled in 2020.
  - 700 m² state-of-the-art facility.
  - Roasts 80,000 to 100,000+ kg annually.
  - Generates high-skill manufacturing jobs in country instead of low-wage manual harvesting alone.

#### 2. FARMERS (Living Incomes Above LIRP)
Moyee pays farmers **20% above the open market rate**, surpassing the **Living Income Reference Price (LIRP)**:
- **Limu, Ethiopia**: Paid **40.52 Birr** per kg cherry. Specialty grade 80+ cupping score.
- **Mizan, Ethiopia**: Paid **38.65 Birr** per kg cherry. 107 farming families overseen by a 10-person management team.
- **Kericho, Kenya**: Paid **78.12 KSh** per kg cherry across 151 smallholder farmers (co-ops: *Kimologit, Kapluso, Koilsir, Tuiyabei*).
- **Mt. Elgon, Uganda**: Paid **14,335 USh** per kg cherry.

#### 3. FORESTS (Regenerative Agroforestry & EUDR Deforestation-Free)
- **Forest-Canopy Grown**: Beans grow under natural forest canopy alongside guava, banana, and native shade trees, preventing deforestation.
- **Carbon Sequestration**: Tree planting and agroforestry sequester **~2,770 tons of CO₂ annually** (~27.7 million kg).
- **Reforestation**: Over **40,000 coffee trees planted annually** (each absorbing ~25 kg CO₂).
- **Biocomposting Revolution**: Begun in 2022 to eliminate synthetic chemical fertilizers; **403,200 kg biocompost produced**, sequestering 100+ tons CO₂ and sustaining 10 permanent jobs.
- **100% Deforestation-Free**: Fully compliant with the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR).

### "Storyproving" & Clean Claims
Moyee eschews unverified greenwashing. In compliance with EU green claim laws, all ecological and social claims are published and verified via the **Clean Claims** management platform and a public real-time dashboard ([https://impactdashboard.moyeecoffee.com/](https://impactdashboard.moyeecoffee.com/)).

## 6. Freshness Engineering & Packaging Innovation

### The "Roast at Origin" Freshness Paradox
- **Myth**: Roasting at origin means beans will go stale during maritime shipping.
- **Reality**:
  1. Traditional Western roasters buy raw green beans that sit in shipping containers, bonded ports, and warehouses for **6 to 24 months** before roasting, losing moisture and vibrancy.
  2. Moyee wet-mills cherries and roasts them **within days of harvest** at origin.
  3. **Nitro-Flush Technology**: Immediately upon roasting and degassing, Moyee seals beans in airtight bags flushed with food-grade nitrogen, displacing 99%+ of oxygen. Without oxygen, cellular oxidation halts completely.
  4. **One-Way Degassing Valve**: Bags feature an engineered one-way membrane that releases CO₂ while blocking atmospheric oxygen.
  5. **Door-to-Door Lead Time**: Coffee arrives at European customer doorsteps within **8 to 12 weeks of roasting**, retaining peak aromatics for years inside unopened nitrogen packaging.
